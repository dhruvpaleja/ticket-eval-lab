    Five independent checks compare each valid output with explicit labels: route, priority, approval, evidence IDs and correct permitted action. Missing or malformed outputs receive no credit and remain in the denominator.

    What replay actually verifies +
    • The exact suite digest matches the candidate file.
    • The supported rubric recomputes every check, total and evaluator event from raw inputs.
    • For built-in simulations, outputs and rule events also reproduce under the pinned simulator version.
    • The canonical payload checksum matches. This is consistency, not a digital signature or proof of authorship.

    Externally supplied labels are separate from candidate decisions; their authorship and quality cannot be established by an importer. These constructed cases are not ITSMBench or evidence of real model performance. No IT actions are executed.
